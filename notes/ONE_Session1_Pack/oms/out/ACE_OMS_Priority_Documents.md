# ACE OMS — Priority Documents and Section Headings

*Acme Caribbean Energy (fictional). Documentation programme for the OMS demonstration. Prepared 21 November 2026.*

This file lists the **30 priority documents** that illustrate OMS at ACE, with the main (top-level) sections each will contain. Framing sections (purpose, scope, abbreviations, definitions, references, document control) are left out; every document will still carry them from the ACE template.

Each heading shows the **OMS tags** it will carry (expectation numbers, most relevant first). Names come from the ACE organisation workbook. Numbers follow the ACE scheme in Part D of the OMS Manual (ACE-AAA-TTT-NNN-SSS).

| Wave | Documents | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Wave 1** | 1–10 | Drive the document management system and the web interface: the documentation rules, the Risk Matrix, the core integrity and control systems, incidents and audit |
| **Wave 2** | 11–20 | Complete the wheel: policy, deviations, competence, contractors, emergency, wells, operating limits, inspection, KPIs, stakeholders |
| **Wave 3** | 21–30 | Show the lower levels and site documents: procedures, a site emergency plan, a register, engineering documents, a work instruction and a form |

**Expectation coverage:** Wave 1 addresses 34 of 54 expectations; Waves 1–2 address 43; all 30 documents address 44. Not yet addressed by any of the 30: E1.2, E1.4, E1.5, E1.6, E2.2, E2.5, E3.2, E3.5, E6.6, E8.5 (these are covered by the OMS Manual and later documents).

**Built-in test faults:** two Wave 1 documents carry deliberate errors so the gateway check has something to catch in the demonstration (see documents 7 and 9).
