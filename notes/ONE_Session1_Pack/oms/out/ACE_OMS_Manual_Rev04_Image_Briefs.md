# OMS Manual Rev 04 — Image briefs

Ten photo placeholders sit in the manual as dashed grey boxes marked **IMAGE IMG-nn**. Each box is 6.5 in wide (16:9 crop works; the box is about 2.2–2.6 in tall, so a wide 21:9 or cropped 16:9 image fits best).

**To replace one in Word:** click inside the grey box › Table Layout › Delete › Delete Table, then Insert › Pictures at the same spot and set the width to 6.5 in. Keep the picture *in line with text*. Add alt text (right-click › View Alt Text).

**Style for all images:** Caribbean light (warm, early morning or golden hour), real-looking industrial settings, people in correct PPE (coveralls, hard hats, safety glasses, gloves; life jackets on boats), no visible brand names or real company logos, a mix of Trinidadian men and women. Colour grade towards ACE teal and orange.
